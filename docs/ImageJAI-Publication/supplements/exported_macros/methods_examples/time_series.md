What this example demonstrates: even on a *synthetic* image with no
acquisition-side metadata at all, the analysis fields (TrackMate
detector + radius + threshold, bleach-correction algorithm, frame
interval and time unit, X×Y×Z×C×T dimensions) auto-populate verbatim.
On a real microscope-acquired time-series the upstream metadata blocks
(microscope, optics, illumination) would also populate from OME-XML,
typically taking the coverage to ~22/33. The synthetic version pins
down the *floor* of what the exporter can promise.
